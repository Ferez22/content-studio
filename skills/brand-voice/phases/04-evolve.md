seo/index.md GSC results.

   Confirmed >= 2x or by results -> also memory/learnings.md.
2. voicing (brand or global): never silent. Proposal in chat: | section | current | proposed | why | data |.
   OK -> apply, bump version (wording/examples: x.y+1; positioning, persona or pillar: x+1.0), changelog line dated.
3. New fact -> brand.md with source tag. Contradicts the source of truth -> flag, the source of truth wins.
4. Monthly review (owner asks, or first session of a month after campaigns): works / stop summary + 1-3 proposed
   adjustments. Commit.
