# P2 Interview -> brands/<b>/sources/interview.md (dated, answers close to verbatim)
Start each block from hypotheses ("Your site says X. Right?"), offer options (A/B/C + other). Low-confidence hypotheses
first. Max 3-4 questions per message. Never ask what analyse.md or imports already answer with high confidence.

## Brand blocks (in order)
1. Offer + model: what you sell, packages, price range, delivery, what you refuse to do.
2. ICP + personas: who buys (role, company size, sector, market), who uses, who blocks. 1-3 personas.
   Propose per persona: desire sold (knowledge/sales/desire-map.md) + buyer type (knowledge/sales/new-vs-old-money.md),
   check knowledge/index.md for other tables -> confirm.
3. Pains + gains per persona: trigger moment, what they tried before, cost of doing nothing.
4. Differentiation: why you vs each competitor in concurrents.md; what only you can claim.
5. Proof: numbers, clients (may we name them?), testimonials, certifications, partners. Tag public | private.
6. Objections: top 5 heard in sales + today's answer.
7. Personality + tone: 3 adjectives, 3 anti-adjectives. Show 3 sample lines in different tones -> pick one.
   Brands or posts you like / dislike (links).
8. Banned words + claims: words you hate, legal limits (no guarantees, regulated claims).
9. Languages + markets: language per market, formality (tu/vous, du/Sie), RTL, terms never translated.
10. Goals + channels: 3-month and 12-month sales goals, main CTA, channels that matter (LinkedIn, site, email, ads...).
11. Audio voice (optional): VO gender, age, accent, energy (ElevenLabs later).

## Global blocks (mode global)
1. Founder voice: 3 posts or texts you are proud of (paste or links), how you open and close, emoji/hashtag use, length.
2. Writing rules for every brand: propose defaults (no em/en dashes, li-human slop list, short paragraphs, concrete
   numbers over adjectives) -> confirm, add, remove.
3. Sales principles: how you sell (advice first? no hard pitch? free call?), what you never promise.
4. Languages you write in + habits per language.

## Revise mode
Only the block concerned; quote the current voicing section first.

End: 10-line recap -> owner confirms -> P3.
