# studio-product-film
Type: product film. Premium short film that makes the product look mature and expensive. Prepend studio-base.
Use when: product awareness, launch hero (site, LinkedIn), boosted brand ad. Product has real UI to show.
Default: 15 s | 16:9 site/LinkedIn, 9:16 social.
Inspired by: prompt-motion.com/daniel-haida-8691d4, /oliahmed205-3e31c4, /samuel-yostt-891f87.

~~~~text
<inputs>
Ask for: product name, one-line promise, the desired viewer reaction in one sentence, 3 real product
moments (screens, objects or flows) in brands/{{brand}}/assets/, the site URL (styling reference),
duration, ratio, music file or "silent + cue sheet".
</inputs>

<story>
Arc: tension -> control -> clarity -> brand. Open on the problem as one strong image, not text.
The 3 product moments carry the film, each its own shot, linked by shared-shape transitions.
End: logo + promise, held long enough to read (>=1.5 s).
</story>

<structure>
0-2 s hook image (problem). 2-11 s three product moments, ~3 s each. 11-13 s the payoff number or result
from brand.md (real only). 13-15 s logo + promise. Scale timings to {{duration}}.
</structure>

<direction>
Film the software like a physical premium object: macro camera, subtle rim light, crisp UI.
Style follows the brand site. Very little copy.
</direction>

<start>
Read brand sources, list the real assets you will use, then show the storyboard before any code.
</start>
~~~~
