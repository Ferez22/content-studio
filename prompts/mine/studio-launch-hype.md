# studio-launch-hype
Type: launch / announcement. Fast, beat-driven, kinetic type + product flashes. Prepend studio-base.
Use when: new feature, new offer, event, milestone. Goal awareness. LinkedIn/IG/X organic or boosted.
Default: 15-20 s | 9:16 (1:1 / 16:9 derivatives).
Inspired by: prompt-motion.com/hqmank-7c61f1, /francoxavier33-d2dfd2, /marouanegazouzi-6071d5, /twoclipping-6dd14e.

~~~~text
<inputs>
Ask for: what is launched, tagline (<=6 words), 3 proof points or features, CTA, launch date, accent color,
2-4 UI captures or visuals, music file with a clear drop or "silent + cue sheet".
</inputs>

<structure>
Bars at the song tempo (default 120 BPM, 2 s per bar).
Bar 1: hook lands word by word on the beats (<3 s, mandatory if boosted).
Bar 2: one hook word morphs into the product or the key visual.
Drop: the reveal, the one big moment.
Then one move per bar: each proof point as big type or a UI flash, one per bar.
Last 2 bars: tagline + CTA + logo, then cut to black.
</structure>

<direction>
Energy from rhythm and cuts on downbeats, not from effects. Kinetic type moves as designed blocks.
One accent color.
</direction>

<start>
Ask for the inputs, then show the storyboard on the beat grid before writing code.
</start>
~~~~
