# Selling to: new money vs old money
Source: sales knowledge gathered from several places (2026-11-05). Table as given (dashes replaced by colons, CLAUDE.md rule 7).

| Dimension | New money | Old money |
|---|---|---|
| Basis of trust | Trusts results more than reputation; will work with someone new who proves they can deliver | Trust already earned over time; reputation and track record come first |
| What they buy | Expertise: people who can solve their problems | Relationships: people who understand their world and become long-term trusted partners |
| Time horizon | Wants faster results and visible progress | Values patience, consistency, decisions that compound over decades |
| How they decide | Fast, individual, gut-driven; convinced by proof of results and moves quickly | Slow, consultative; runs it past advisors and family, weighs downside and reputation risk |
| Response to outreach | Open to cold contact; replies if the offer is concrete and the proof is upfront | Cold contact mostly ignored; responds to warm intros and referrals from inside the network |
| What closes the deal | Demonstrated outcome: case study, numbers, a quick win they can see | Credibility over time: who vouches for you, discretion, and fit with how they already operate |
| Status motive | Wants access to higher-status environments | Already has access; looks for people who add value to the existing network |
