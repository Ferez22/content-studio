**Choosing a CRM is rarely the hard part. Getting value from it is.** In a 2025 Bain & Company survey of more than 1,200 senior commercial executives, 70% said their companies fail to properly build their sales methods into tools like their CRM, and only about 20% had realised the full value of those tools ([Bain & Company](https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/20252/70-of-companies-struggle-to-integrate-their-sales-plays-into-crm-and-revenue-technologies-finds-bain--company-survey/)).

So pick the CRM your team will actually use, at a price that still makes sense in year two. A CRM (customer relationship management system) is the software where your team keeps track of contacts, companies and deals. Here's how to choose one in about five minutes.

## Four questions to answer first

1. **How many people will use it, and what can you spend per person?** Prices are per user per month, so ten users multiply everything by ten. Check the price for year two as well: some offers are first-year discounts.
2. **Do you only sell, or do you also invoice and manage stock?** If it's sales only, a sales CRM is simpler. If the back office should live in the same system, look at Odoo or Dynamics 365.
3. **Which language, and where must the data live?** If your team works in Arabic, or your sector requires data to stay in the region, check this before price (see below).
4. **Who will set it up and look after it?** Some CRMs run well out of the box. In our experience, the most customisable ones, like Salesforce and Dynamics 365, need an admin or a partner to get right.

## Each CRM in a few lines

<Logo tool="hubspot" />

### HubSpot

HubSpot is the easiest place to start. You get the free plan for up to two users, and Starter costs $20 per seat a month, though promotions often run ([HubSpot pricing](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales)). Gartner lists it as a Niche Player for sales force automation, aimed mostly at small and mid-size businesses ([CX Today](https://www.cxtoday.com/marketing-sales-technology/gartner-magic-quadrant-for-sales-force-automation-platforms-sfa-2025-the-rundown/)). Watch the step up to Professional, though: it's $90 per seat a month billed annually, plus a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee. Arabic is not one of its interface languages ([HubSpot](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/help-and-resources/hubspot-language-offerings)).

<Logo tool="salesforce" />

### Salesforce

If you need deep customisation, you'll end up looking at Salesforce. It's the most customisable option here and a Leader in Gartner's 2025 Magic Quadrant for sales force automation ([CX Today](https://www.cxtoday.com/marketing-sales-technology/gartner-magic-quadrant-for-sales-force-automation-platforms-sfa-2025-the-rundown/)). Starter Suite costs $25 per user a month, Pro Suite $100, and the core plans run from $195 to $550, mostly on annual contracts ([Salesforce pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/pricing/)). Lower tiers carry limits, such as 5 automated flows on Pro Suite and API access as a $25 add-on. Arabic covers standard pages but not the setup pages ([Salesforce Help](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=xcloud.faq_getstart_what_languages_does.htm&type=5)).

<Logo tool="zoho" />

### Zoho CRM

Zoho CRM is free for up to 3 users, then $14, $23 or $40 per user a month billed annually ([Zoho pricing](https://www.zoho.com/en-us/crm/zohocrm-pricing.html)). It's the strongest regional fit in this list: Arabic (Egypt) is fully supported ([Zoho](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/translations/articles/languages-supported-in-zoho-crm)), its data centres include Riyadh, Jeddah, Dubai and Abu Dhabi ([Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/know-your-datacenter.html)), and it has offices in Dubai, Riyadh, Cairo, Cape Town, Lagos and Nairobi ([Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/contactus.html)). Testing tools like the developer sandbox come only with Enterprise.

### Pipedrive

Pipedrive calls itself "a CRM built by salespeople, for salespeople" ([Pipedrive](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/about)), and you'll learn its visual pipeline in an afternoon. There's no free plan, only a 14-day trial. Lite starts at $14 per seat a month billed annually and Growth at $39 ([Pipedrive pricing](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/pricing)). Lead capture, email campaigns and website visitor tracking are paid add-ons. Its right-to-left support covers typed text only, not the layout ([Pipedrive](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/does-pipedrive-support-right-to-left-language-input)).

<Logo tool="odoo" />

### Odoo

We'd pick Odoo when sales and the back office belong together: its Standard plan includes CRM, invoicing, accounting and inventory for one price ([Odoo pricing](https://www.odoo.com/pricing)). It costs $24.90 per user a month billed yearly, but that's a 12-month discount for the users in your first order; the normal price is $31.10. Customisation and the external API need the Custom plan, and Odoo's own implementation packs start at $493 for 4 hours ([Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/pricing-packs)). It supports Arabic with a right-to-left layout ([Odoo docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/administration/on_premise/source.html)) and offers hosting in Saudi Arabia ([Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/privacy)).

### Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales

If your company already runs on Microsoft 365 and Teams, Dynamics 365 Sales is worth a look. Microsoft says it has been a Leader in Gartner's Magic Quadrant for sales force automation for 15 years in a row ([Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics-365/blog/business-leader/2025/07/30/microsoft-named-a-leader-in-the-2025-gartner-magic-quadrant-for-sales-force-automation-platforms/)). Sales Professional costs $65 per user a month, Enterprise $105 and Premium $150, all paid yearly, with no free plan ([Microsoft pricing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics-365/products/sales/pricing)). Microsoft's 2025 licensing guide limits the entry tier to 15 custom tables per app ([Microsoft](https://cdn-dynmedia-1.microsoft.com/is/content/microsoftcorp/microsoft/final/en-us/microsoft-brand/documents/Dynamics-365-Licensing-Guide-June-2025.pdf)). Data can be hosted in the UAE or South Africa ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/new-datacenter-regions)).

### monday CRM

If your team already plans its work in monday.com, you'll feel at home in monday CRM. Basic costs $12 per seat a month billed yearly, Standard $17 and Pro $28, with a minimum of 3 seats and yearly plans paid upfront ([monday pricing](https://monday.com/crm/pricing)). The cheaper tiers are tight: Basic caps you at 1,000 active contacts and deals. Gartner added it in 2025 as a Niche Player and noted limited AI depth ([CX Today](https://www.cxtoday.com/marketing-sales-technology/gartner-magic-quadrant-for-sales-force-automation-platforms-sfa-2025-the-rundown/)).

### Freshsales

If budget is your first filter, look at Freshsales from Freshworks. It's the cheapest paid entry here at $9 per user a month billed annually, with Pro at $39 and Enterprise at $59 ([Freshworks pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/pricing/)). Its AI agent is billed separately, at $49 per 100 sessions. Freshworks announced a UAE data centre in 2024 ([Freshworks](https://www.freshworks.com/press-releases/freshworks-unveils-new-data-center-in-uae-to-serve-customers-across-middle-east-and-africa/)), but it dropped out of Gartner's 2025 Magic Quadrant for sales force automation ([CX Today](https://www.cxtoday.com/marketing-sales-technology/gartner-magic-quadrant-for-sales-force-automation-platforms-sfa-2025-the-rundown/)).

## Arabic and where your data lives

We'd check this before price. If your team works in Arabic, or your sector says the data must stay in the region, half the list drops out.

- **Arabic interface:** Odoo (right-to-left) and Zoho CRM (Arabic Egypt fully, other variants partly). Salesforce translates standard pages only, with right-to-left limitations. HubSpot has none.
- **Hosting in the Gulf:** Zoho (Saudi Arabia and the UAE), Odoo (Saudi Arabia) and Dynamics 365 (the UAE, and South Africa further south). Salesforce lists the UAE, but since 20 October 2026 it routes UAE-hosted accounts to the EU "due to the ongoing situation in the Middle East" ([Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/en-us/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/documents/legal/misc/salesforce-infrastructure-and-subprocessors.pdf)).
- **No Middle East or African region:** HubSpot ([HubSpot](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/account-security/hubspot-cloud-infrastructure-and-data-hosting-frequently-asked-questions)), Pipedrive ([Pipedrive](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/nova-faq)) and monday ([monday](https://monday.com/trustcenter/privacy/overview)).

## Our short answer

If you asked us today, we'd say:

- **First CRM, small team, small budget:** Zoho CRM or HubSpot's free plan.
- **A sales team that lives in its pipeline:** Pipedrive.
- **Sales plus invoicing and stock:** Odoo.
- **A large or complex sales organisation:** Salesforce, or Dynamics 365 if you already run on Microsoft.
- **Already on monday.com:** monday CRM.

Whichever you pick, the setup decides the result. HubSpot's own data shows customers who work with an implementation partner close 4x more deals than those who don't. That's a correlation in vendor data, not proof, but it matches what we see ([HubSpot ROI Report 2025](https://53.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/53/Data%20Assets%20Repository/ROI%20Report%202025/HubSpot%20ROI%20Report%202025.pdf)). ## FAQ

### What is the best CRM for a small business?

For most small teams, HubSpot's free plan (up to 2 users), Zoho CRM's free plan (up to 3 users) or Pipedrive (from $14 per seat a month, billed annually) are the usual starting points. Choose Zoho if you need Arabic or data hosted in Saudi Arabia or the UAE, Pipedrive if your team mainly works a sales pipeline, and HubSpot if marketing and sales should share one tool.

### Is there a CRM with an Arabic interface?

Yes. Odoo supports Arabic with a right-to-left layout, and Zoho CRM fully supports Arabic (Egypt), with other Arabic variants partly supported. Salesforce translates standard pages into Arabic but not its setup pages. HubSpot has no Arabic interface, and Pipedrive's right-to-left support covers typed text only.

### How much does a CRM cost?

In October 2026, paid entry plans ran from $9 (Freshsales) to $65 (Dynamics 365 Sales Professional) per user a month, and top plans reached $550 (Salesforce Max). Budget for setup too: HubSpot charges a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee on Professional, and Odoo's implementation packs start at $493.

### Should our CRM also handle invoicing and stock?

If you also invoice, buy and keep stock, Odoo puts the CRM and those back office apps in one system for one price per user, and Dynamics 365 does the same inside the Microsoft world. If you only need to track contacts and deals, a sales CRM like HubSpot, Pipedrive or Zoho is simpler to start with.
